# Code of Conduct

PixelQuota welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Be respectful, discuss the work rather than attacking people, give actionable technical feedback, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, or deliberately disruptive behavior.

Project spaces are for improving PixelQuota and helping its users. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these expectations, and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project useful and safe.

If a conduct issue involves private or sensitive information, use a private GitHub contact mechanism rather than posting it publicly.
